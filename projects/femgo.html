MgO Miscibility in Liquid Iron

Density Functional Theory • Geodynamo • Earth Evolution

View Publication

This project investigates the miscibility of MgO in liquid iron under conditions relevant to Earth's early differentiation. Density Functional Theory (DFT) calculations were performed using VASP to explore lithophile-core interactions and their implications for the chemical evolution of Earth's interior.

Atomic-scale simulations were used to characterize the interface between liquid iron and MgO-rich phases, providing insight into the thermodynamic behavior of mantle-derived components during core formation.

Research Objectives

  • Characterize the liquid Fe–MgO two-phase interface.
  • Investigate lithophile element partitioning into metallic liquids.
  • Develop a thermodynamic framework for Fe–MgO phase behavior.
  • Assess implications for Earth's early geodynamo.
